GOOGLE PLAY COMPLIANCE POLICY

Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Matrimonyer (Package: matrimonyapp.matrimonyer.com) • Plestar Inc

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Absolute Zero Tolerance Commitment:

Matrimonyer strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), child endangerment, grooming, and the facilitation of underage marriage. Any violation results in immediate, permanent account termination and referral to law enforcement and international reporting authorities.

1. Purpose & Application Scope

This Child Safety Standards Policy applies to all users, accounts, uploaded photographs, biographical data, and communications within the Matrimonyer mobile application (Application ID: matrimonyapp.matrimonyer.com), operated by Plestar Inc.

Matrimonyer is a dedicated matrimonial matchmaking platform exclusively intended for lawful adults seeking matrimony. It is not a casual dating app or a minor-accessible social network.

2. Strict Age Requirement (18+ Only)

Age Restrictions & Technical Controls:
  • Users must be at least 18 years of age (female) or 21 years of age (male) under applicable marriage laws to register.
  • Our in-app date of birth pickers physically restrict dates to individuals who are 18 years of age or older.
  • Backend API endpoints strictly validate date-of-birth timestamps and automatically reject any registration attempt representing a person under 18 years of age.
  • Accounts created for or on behalf of minors are strictly forbidden and terminated immediately upon discovery.

3. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)

We enforce an absolute zero-tolerance policy against:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), including digital, photographic, video, or AI-generated depictions of child abuse or exploitation.
  • Online child sexual exploitation, sexual extortion, solicitation of minors, or child grooming behaviors.
  • Arranging, promoting, or facilitating child marriage, underage unions, or trafficking of minors.
  • Any user content or communication that sexualizes, endangers, or harms children in any manner.

4. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Matrimonyer empowers all users to actively report concerns, suspicious behavior, or safety violations:

How to Report In-App:
1. On any candidate profile, tap the Report Profile or overflow options icon.
2. Select the report category (e.g., "Suspected Underage / Child Safety Concern" or "Inappropriate Content").
3. Submit details. Our safety moderation team reviews reported accounts urgently.

Users may also immediately escalate child safety concerns by emailing our dedicated point of contact at: anil@plestar.net or support@plestarinc.com.

5. Content Moderation & Investigation Protocols

We maintain comprehensive safeguards to identify and neutralize policy violations:

  • Automated & Manual Review: Uploaded profile pictures are screened for appropriate content. Photographs depicting minors or inappropriate imagery are immediately blocked.
  • Immediate Account Suspension: Any account suspected of involvement with CSAM, CSAE, or minor exploitation is immediately frozen and all access revoked pending investigation.
  • Evidence Preservation: When child sexual abuse material or exploitation is detected, relevant account metadata, IP logs, and evidence are securely retained for law enforcement handover.

6. Mandatory Reporting to Law Enforcement & Authorities

Matrimonyer complies with all applicable local, national, and international child protection laws. In accordance with legal obligations and Google Play safety policies:

  • All instances of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or exploitation identified on our platform are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline.
  • We actively cooperate with national cybercrime divisions (including the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal in India at cybercrime.gov.in) and relevant statutory authorities worldwide.
  • We respond promptly to lawful court orders, subpoenas, and investigative requests from law enforcement agencies.
