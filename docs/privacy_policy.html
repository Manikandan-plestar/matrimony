1. Introduction

Welcome to Matrimonyer ("we," "our," or "us"), provided by Plestar Inc. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Matrimonyer mobile application (Application ID: matrimonyapp.matrimonyer.com) and associated matchmaking services.

By creating an account or accessing Matrimonyer, you consent to the collection, transfer, storage, and handling of your data as detailed in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide genuine matchmaking and verify account authenticity, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Credentials: Email address for secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification and authentication.
  • Matrimonial Profile Details (Biodata): Full name, gender, date of birth, marital status, mother tongue, religion, community/caste, education, occupation, annual income, height, and partner expectations.
  • User-Uploaded Photographs: Profile pictures and gallery photos uploaded by you to depict yourself accurately.
  • Contact Information: Mobile telephone number, used for verified member communication under protected privacy settings.
  • Purchase & Subscription Information: When you purchase the Premium Membership (₹499/year), transaction order IDs and subscription tokens generated by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. We do not store credit/debit card numbers directly on our servers.

3. Location Data & Permissions (Prominent Disclosure)

Location Usage Disclosure:
Matrimonyer requests access to device location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). This permission is accessed strictly in the foreground when you set up your profile or search for matches, solely to reverse-geocode your approximate City, State, and Country.

We DO NOT: track your real-time GPS coordinates continuously, collect background location while the app is closed, or share your live geographic coordinates with other users.

4. Camera & Storage Permissions

We request access to your device camera and photo gallery (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) solely when you choose to capture or upload profile photographs. Photos are compressed, safely transmitted via encrypted SSL/TLS, and stored securely.

5. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for legitimate matrimonial purposes:

  • To calculate compatibility scores and display relevant candidate recommendations.
  • To provide core matchmaking features, including profile visitor insights, saved profiles, and expressed interests.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud, fake profiles, harassment, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To verify payments and manage your 1-Year Premium Membership.

6. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We enforce a strict Zero Third-Party Data Sale policy:

  • We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information, biodata, or photos to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, or advertisers.
  • Service Providers: We share data only with trusted cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., secure database servers, email delivery services) bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable Indian law or valid legal process.

7. Contact Number Protection

Your mobile phone number is protected. Users can customize their privacy controls in Settings ➔ Protect Mobile Number to ensure contact numbers are only displayed to mutual, verified connections.

8. Account Deletion & Data Retention Policy (Right to be Forgotten)

Instant Permanent Deletion:
You have the right to delete your account at any time. Inside the application, navigate to Settings ➔ Delete My Account.

Upon confirmation:
  • Your profile is immediately wiped and removed from all discovery decks and feeds.
  • All uploaded photos are permanently deleted from active storage.
  • Your visitor history, mutual matches, chat records, and preferences are permanently deleted.
You may also submit a deletion request via email to: support@plestarinc.com.

9. Children & Minors Policy

Matrimonyer is strictly for individuals who are legally eligible for marriage (18+ for females, 21+ for males under Indian Law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age. Accounts found belonging to minors will be terminated immediately.

10. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3), hashed passwords/tokens, strict firewalls, and role-based database access to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
